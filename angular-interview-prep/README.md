## Suggested revision plan
1. **Day −3:** skim sections A and D of files 01, 02 and 05, the areas most likely to be probed in depth. Mark every question you couldn't answer out loud in under 2 minutes.
2. **Day −2:** do tasks 1, 2, 3, 5 and 8 from file 11 against a timer. Go through file 10 and fill in your story bank with real numbers.
3. **Day −1:** read the trap questions (section B) in every file, run a mock interview, then revisit your weak areas.
4. **Interview day:** read file 12 (the cheatsheet) and pick 3–4 questions from `12-questions-to-ask-them.md`.
